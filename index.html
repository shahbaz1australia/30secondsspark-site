What this is

30SecondSpark is a daily short-video channel. Each video takes one idea — a psychology finding, a line from a classic book, a short parable, a piece of practical wisdom from a culture around the world — and tells it as a thirty-second story. No lecture, no filler: one idea, told well, in the time it takes to read this sentence twice.

The channel publishes to YouTube Shorts and TikTok under the same name and handle on both platforms.

About the publisher

30SecondSpark is a short-form video publisher. Every episode is researched, written, produced and published in-house, and released free to the public on YouTube and TikTok under the 30SecondSpark name. The channel has published over 400 episodes to date and releases new ones daily.

The 30SecondSpark publishing application connects the channel's production system to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts so each finished episode is delivered to its audience automatically on release. It distributes 30SecondSpark's own original programming to the public — it is not an internal business tool, and it does not act on behalf of any third party.
