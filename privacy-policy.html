Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

30SecondSpark ("the channel", "we") publishes short videos to YouTube and TikTok. This policy explains what account data we access through those platforms' publishing APIs, and how we handle it.

What we connect to

30SecondSpark uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API to publish videos to the channel's own TikTok (@30secondsspark) and YouTube (@30secondsspark) accounts. We do not operate on behalf of any other account or user.

What data we access

Through TikTok's OAuth, we request:

  • user.info.basic — basic public profile info (display name, avatar, open ID) of the connected account, used only to confirm we're publishing to the correct account.
  • video.upload / video.publish — permission to upload and publish video files to the connected account.

Through Google/YouTube OAuth, we request equivalent upload-only permission for the channel's own YouTube account, to publish videos and set their title, description and category.

We do not request or access TikTok/YouTube direct messages, followers, analytics beyond what the publishing APIs return, or any data belonging to other accounts.

How we use it

Access tokens are used solely to upload and publish the channel's own videos. We do not use this access to read, modify or delete any other content on the connected accounts, and we do not use it for anything beyond publishing.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the machine that runs the publishing software and are never transmitted to any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent or share account data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.
  • We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only as long as needed to continue publishing. You (the account owner) can revoke access at any time from TikTok's Manage Account → "Authorized apps" settings, or from Google Account → Security → Third-party access for YouTube. Revoking access immediately stops any further publishing.

Children's privacy

30SecondSpark's publishing tooling is operated by its author and is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
